Meet Miss Kay

A teacher with a passion for little minds and big possibilities.

Hi, I’m Miss Kay!

My journey in early childhood education is rooted in my love for children, creativity and the importance of building strong foundations during the early years. I have completed my Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE) training, achieving full academic marks across my examinations and assignments.

My additional certifications include Child Psychology, Autistic Children in School Environments, Play Therapy and First Aid in Child Care. Alongside my academic training, I have gained practical teaching experience and have worked with children from underprivileged communities through summer learning activities.

My passion extends beyond the classroom. I create original educational books, learning resources and structured educational programmes designed to make learning engaging, meaningful and accessible for young children.

Through Miss Kay’s Learning Studio, I bring together my knowledge of early childhood development, creativity and hands-on teaching to support children as they build confidence, develop essential skills and discover the joy of learning.

I believe every child deserves the opportunity to learn, explore and shine — and I’m here to help make that journey a little brighter.
